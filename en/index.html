What I do

01

Process automation

Wire your daily repeats into something that runs on its own — email triage, data pulls, report generation, content distribution. n8n, agent orchestration, or plain scripts. You get a workflow that keeps running, not a one-off demo.

02

// 02 — about

About

Someone who builds things with AI and hardware

I'm ZS. The past few years I've been bouncing between hardware engineering and AI tooling: commissioning stage machinery on site, running consumer hardware through its full lifecycle, and now using large models to standardize repetitive work. More scars than lines of code.

That gave me two things: I can read a shop floor, and I can write code. So this is how I work — find out what you actually need first, then build. If it doesn't run, I keep fixing until it does.
